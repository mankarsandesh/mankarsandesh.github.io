01 / A bit about me

A developer. Always a builder.

I'm a full-stack developer with over 7 years of hands-on experience building web applications from the ground up. What excites me most is taking an idea from concept to reality – I've built everything from survey platforms to video-sharing apps to finance management tools, often starting with just a blank canvas and a problem to solve.

Throughout my career, I've had the opportunity to work across different countries – India, Malaysia, Laos, and now the UK. Each experience taught me something new, whether it was managing government projects in Laos, leading teams at Indian startups, or building enterprise solutions remotely for international clients.

I genuinely enjoy the challenge of taking messy requirements and turning them into clean, working software. Whether it's optimizing database queries, designing intuitive user interfaces, or building APIs that handle thousands of requests – I love the problem-solving aspect of development.

Now based in the UK, I'm looking to join a forward-thinking company where I can contribute my experience building products from scratch, work with talented teams, and continue growing as an engineer.
